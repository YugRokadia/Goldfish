GoldfishSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

GoldfishSearch is a Windows desktop application designed to help users search and organize information available on their device and connected services.

Local Data

GoldfishSearch is designed to process indexed local information on the user's device. Local index data is stored on the user's computer.

Google Gmail

If a user chooses to connect a Google account, GoldfishSearch may access Gmail data through the Google Gmail API.

Gmail access is used to allow the user to search and view relevant email information within GoldfishSearch.

GoldfishSearch does not use Gmail access to send, modify, or delete emails.

Gmail data accessed through the Google API is not sold or used for advertising.

Third-Party Services

GoldfishSearch may connect to third-party services when explicitly authorized by the user. Each service's own privacy policy and terms may also apply.

Data Sharing

GoldfishSearch does not sell users' personal information.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or GoldfishSearch, please contact the project maintainers through the project's GitHub repository.
